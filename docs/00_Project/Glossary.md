# Glossary

This document defines the main business and technical terms used in Beyraghe Mandegar.

---

# Product Terms

## Show

نمایش / اثر هنری

A complete artistic work performed by the group.

Example:

- Macbeth
- Hamlet

---

## Performance

اجرا / سانس مشخص

A scheduled occurrence of a Show at a specific date, time, and venue.

A Show can have multiple Performances.

Example:
Show:
Macbeth
Performance:
20 Azar 1405
19:30
Farhangsara Khavaran

---

## Venue

سالن اجرا

The physical location where a Performance takes place.

Includes:

- Name
- Address
- Coordinates
- Navigation links

---

# Reservation Terms

## Customer

مخاطب / مشتری

A person who uses the platform to reserve tickets.

Identified primarily by mobile number.

---

## OTP (One-Time Password)

کد یکبار مصرف

A verification mechanism used to authenticate customer mobile numbers.

---

## Reservation

رزرو

A customer's request to reserve one or more tickets for a specific Performance.

---

## Ticket

بلیت دیجیتال

A digital admission document generated from a confirmed reservation.

Contains:

- Ticket ID
- QR Code
- Validation status

---

## QR Code

A secure machine-readable code used for ticket validation during check-in.

---

## Check-in

ثبت ورود

The process of validating a ticket and recording audience attendance.

---

# Sales Terms

## Sales Window

بازه فروش

The period during which ticket reservations are available.

Defined by:

- Start Date/Time
- End Date/Time

---

## Capacity

ظرفیت

The maximum number of available admissions.

Capacity exists at multiple levels:

- Venue Capacity
- Performance Capacity
- Sellable Capacity

---

## Purchase Limit

محدودیت خرید

Maximum or minimum number of tickets allowed in a single reservation.

---

# Administration Terms

## Admin

مدیر سیستم

A user who manages the platform.

---

## Operator

اپراتور اجرا

A user responsible for operational tasks such as QR check-in.

---

## Dashboard

داشبورد مدیریتی

A visual interface showing operational metrics.

Examples:

- Reservations
- Attendance
- Sales status

---

# Technical Terms

## Notification Service

A system component responsible for sending customer communications.

Examples:

- SMS OTP
- Reservation confirmation
- Reminder messages

---

## Audit Log

ثبت تاریخچه فعالیت‌ها

A record of important administrative actions.

---

## RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)

کنترل دسترسی مبتنی بر نقش

A security model where permissions are assigned based on user roles.
