# Beyraghe Mandegar - Project Overview

## Product Name

Beyraghe Mandegar

## Overview

Beyraghe Mandegar is a digital event management platform designed for an artistic group to manage performances, audience reservations, digital tickets, and event-day operations.

The platform provides a complete lifecycle management system for artistic events:

- Managing artistic works (Shows)
- Managing performance schedules (Performances)
- Managing venues
- Managing audience reservations
- Creating digital tickets
- QR-based check-in
- Customer communication
- Operational dashboards and reports

The platform combines a public-facing website for audiences with a professional administration system for the artistic team.

---

# Objectives

The main objectives of Beyraghe Mandegar are:

## Audience Experience

- Provide a simple Persian-first reservation experience.
- Allow users to reserve tickets using mobile OTP authentication.
- Provide personal ticket management.
- Enable digital ticket sharing and printing.
- Provide easy navigation to performance venues.

## Operational Management

- Enable administrators to manage shows and performances.
- Control ticket sales windows.
- Monitor reservations and attendance.
- Manage event-day check-in operations.
- Provide operational dashboards.

## Business Growth

- Create a digital identity for the artistic group.
- Preserve historical performance archives.
- Build a foundation for future ticketing capabilities.
