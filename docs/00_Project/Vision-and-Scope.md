# Vision and Scope

## Vision

Beyraghe Mandegar aims to become a reliable digital platform for artistic groups to manage their performances and create a professional connection with their audiences.

The platform should simplify the complete journey:

Audience:

Discover → Reserve → Receive Ticket → Attend

Team:

Create Event → Manage Sales → Control Entry → Analyze Results

---

# Product Principles

## Simple

The system should remain easy to use for both audiences and administrators.

## Professional

The platform should provide operational capabilities expected from professional event management systems.

## Mobile First

Most activities happen on mobile devices:

- Audience reservation
- Ticket access
- QR check-in
- Admin operations

## Security by Design

Security must be considered from the beginning:

- Secure authentication
- Protected APIs
- Data privacy
- Auditability

---

# Scope

## In Scope

### Event Management

- Show management
- Performance scheduling
- Venue management

### Reservation

- Mobile OTP authentication
- Ticket quantity control
- Reservation lifecycle
- Sales windows

### Ticketing

- Digital tickets
- QR codes
- Offline validation support

### Operations

- Check-in
- Attendance tracking
- Dashboards

### Communication

- SMS verification
- Reservation notifications
- Reminder messages

### Public Website

- Upcoming performances
- Past performances archive
- News
- Gallery

---

# Out of Scope

Currently excluded:

- Online payment
- Seat selection
- Marketplace
- Multi-organization SaaS
- Accounting system
- Contract management

These features may be considered in future phases.

---

# Future Expansion

Potential future capabilities:

- Seat map management
